# Cofounder

A two-person entertainment test that gives prospective or current AI startup cofounders a shared team identity and surfaces structural differences worth discussing. It provokes with compatibility language but does not decide whether two people should work together.

## Language

**Pair**:
The two participants who complete one shared test and receive one shared result.
_Avoid_: Team, session, couple

**Participant**:
One verified Account occupying one of the two places in a Pair. The same Account may participate in multiple Pairs, but answers independently within each one.
_Avoid_: User A, User B, founder

**Account**:
A recoverable, passwordless identity owned through a verified email address. An Account is not a Pair and may participate in more than one Pair.
_Avoid_: Participant, profile, email address

**Pair Test**:
The complete two-part assessment answered independently by both Participants.
_Avoid_: Personality test, compatibility verdict, psychological assessment

**Diagnostics Application**:
The single IE-style application in which a Participant completes the Pair Test and views the Pair result.
_Avoid_: Showcase, questionnaire window, test app

**Invitation Link**:
The single-use link through which an authenticated Account explicitly claims the unoccupied place in a Pair. It does not grant access to either Participant's answers or report.
_Avoid_: Sign-in link, report link, shared result link

**Private Report**:
The Pair-only interpretation of dimension scores, complementary traits, structural differences, and recommended conversations. It has no overall compatibility score and does not expose raw answers to Sensitive Topics.
_Avoid_: Diagnosis, verdict, answer sheet

**Public Identity Card**:
A deliberately shareable, non-sensitive expression of a Pair's archetype, strengths, and team identity. It does not display a numerical compatibility score.
_Avoid_: Private Report, score card, diagnostic report

**Public Archetype**:
A share-safe, identity-affirming classification selected from a finite Content Library. It never reveals or implies a Sensitive Topic.
_Avoid_: Private Risk Pattern, personality type

**Private Risk Pattern**:
A Pair-only classification that names a structural risk or dysfunctional tendency for discussion. It is never published by default.
_Avoid_: Public Archetype, diagnosis

**Identity Receipt**:
The receipt-shaped form of the Public Identity Card, revealed through a printer animation after the Pair result is generated.
_Avoid_: Report, invoice, certificate

**Public Result Page**:
The shareable web page behind an Identity Receipt, with social preview metadata and a path for a visitor to start a new Pair Test.
_Avoid_: Private Report, receipt image

**Sensitive Topic**:
A subject whose individual answer is never attributed in the Private Report or included in the Public Identity Card, including equity, ethics, commitment or exit, acquisition intent, and CEO removal.
_Avoid_: Red flag

**Sensitive Signal**:
A non-attributed Conflict Flag or Unresolved Topic derived from both Participants' Sensitive Topic answers after Pair Completion. It cannot reveal either Participant's original option.
_Avoid_: Sensitive answer, raw answer

**Structural Difference**:
A consequential disagreement surfaced for discussion without implying that either Participant is wrong.
_Avoid_: Incompatibility, defect, failure

**Conflict Flag**:
A versioned, deterministic Rules output marking a consequential Pair-level conflict. It may be derived from ordinary answers or retained as a non-attributed Sensitive Signal, but it is never invented by Jev.
_Avoid_: Low score, diagnosis, Jev insight

**Dimension Match**:
A 0–100 score describing the Pair's relationship on one named dimension under that dimension's versioned rule. It is never combined into an overall compatibility score.
_Avoid_: Pair score, compatibility score, Final Score

**Unresolved Topic**:
A consequential subject that one or both Participants report they have not discussed. It is a conversation priority, not evidence of disagreement.
_Avoid_: Conflict Flag, incompatibility, missing answer

**Pair Feature Vector**:
A de-identified, versioned set of rule-derived Pair characteristics used for bounded classification and ranking. It contains neither raw answers nor Account identifiers.
_Avoid_: Answers, report, prompt

**Narrative Priority**:
A categorical product prior describing how strongly a dimension should influence result storytelling. It does not change a Participant's dimension value or Dimension Match.
_Avoid_: Score weight, compatibility weight

**Content Library**:
The versioned collection of human-authored report modules, insight text, conversation prompts, and public identity copy selected by stable content identifiers.
_Avoid_: Generated copy, prompt library

**Classification Result**:
The immutable, versioned set of archetype, severity, insight, and conversation-prompt content identifiers selected for a Pair, together with the classifier's confidence and provenance.
_Avoid_: Generated report, AI response

**Conservative Result**:
A complete Classification Result produced only by deterministic Rules when the external classifier is unavailable or insufficiently confident.
_Avoid_: Error report, partial report

**Pair Completion**:
The point at which both Participants have independently submitted the Pair Test and the shared result can be generated.
_Avoid_: Test completion, user completion

**Report Ready**:
The point at which an immutable Classification Result and its referenced Content Library version are available to both Participants.
_Avoid_: Pair Completion, test submitted

**Mirror Accuracy**:
The exact-match count from six Mirror Answers, presented alongside separately counted near and opposite predictions and an interpretation band rather than a percentage.
_Avoid_: Compatibility score, familiarity percentage

**Pair Share Rate**:
The proportion of created Pairs that reach a recorded share action for their Public Result Page or Identity Receipt.
_Avoid_: Result Share Rate, completion rate

**Participant Withdrawal**:
A Participant's deletion of their own answers. Withdrawal invalidates all Pair results and Public Identity Cards derived from those answers without deleting the other Participant's answers.
_Avoid_: Pair deletion, account deletion

**Account Deletion**:
The deletion of an Account and a Participant Withdrawal from every Pair in which it participated. It invalidates those shared results while preserving the other Participants' independently provided data.
_Avoid_: Pair deletion, Participant Withdrawal

**Withdrawn Result**:
The non-identifying tombstone shown at a former Public Result Page after Participant Withdrawal. It reveals no Pair name, archetype, score, or receipt.
_Avoid_: Deleted Pair, expired result

**Expired Pair**:
An incomplete Pair deleted after 30 consecutive days without valid Participant activity.
_Avoid_: Withdrawn Result, completed Pair
