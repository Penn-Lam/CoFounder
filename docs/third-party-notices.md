## Fusion Pixel fonts

The Diagnostics interface embeds character-subsetted builds of:

- [Fusion Pixel Font](https://github.com/TakWolf/fusion-pixel-font), version 2026.09.01
- [Fusion Bold Pixel Font](https://github.com/pixel-font-studio/fusion-bold-pixel-font), version 2026.09.01

Both font projects are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Their
copyright notices and license texts are included beside the fonts as
`FusionPixel-Regular-OFL.txt` and `FusionPixel-Bold-OFL.txt`.

The embedded WOFF2 files use the upstream 12px proportional Simplified Chinese
builds and retain only the Chinese characters currently used by the application,
plus Latin, punctuation, arrows, symbols, and full-width forms. Unsupported
characters fall back to the platform font.
