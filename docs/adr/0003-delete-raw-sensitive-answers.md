# Delete raw Sensitive Topic answers after Pair Completion

Encrypt each Participant's raw Sensitive Topic options while a Pair remains incomplete. At Pair Completion, deterministic Rules derive only the non-attributed Sensitive Signals and immutable report inputs needed by the product, then delete the original options. Operators receive anonymized aggregates rather than individual sensitive choices, and Jev never receives those raw answers.

This deliberately gives up later inspection and rescoring of equity, ethics, commitment, acquisition-intent, and CEO-removal answers. The trade-off is warranted because reports are versioned and immutable, while retaining who selected which sensitive option would add breach and misuse risk without enabling a required product behavior.
